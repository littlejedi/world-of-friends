# Shanghai Visual References

The linked photographs are research references only. They are not redistributed with the game and are not used as textures. All game imagery must remain original.

## Wukang Mansion

Sources:

- [Official Shanghai travel listing](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/en/xuhui-district/wukang-mansion-428331/)
- [SmartShanghai venue photography](https://www.smartshanghai.com/venue/14908/wukang_mansion_huaihai_zhong_lu)

Traits carried into the art study:

- Long, narrow ship-like wedge at the street junction.
- Red-brick upper façade divided into strong horizontal bands.
- Stone-colored pedestal with continuous ground-floor arcades.
- Repeated tall windows, iron balconies, and a narrow pointed roof termination.
- Mature plane trees, cross streets, pedestrian paving, and dense everyday street detail.

## Xuhui Riverside

Sources:

- [Official Shanghai Xuhui Riverside / West Bund listing](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/en/xuhui-district/xuhui-riverside-west-bund-733955/)
- [SmartShanghai riverside park photography](https://www.smartshanghai.com/venue/6685/Xuhui_Riverside_Public_Open_Space)

Traits carried into the art study:

- Wide, multi-use promenade immediately beside the Huangpu River.
- Red running lane, pale paving, metal railing, seating, lawns, and planted shade.
- Preserved orange loading cranes and industrial structures as visual anchors.
- Boats and barges, open water reflections, distant bridges, and modern skyline.
- More open sky and horizontal breathing room than the compact Wukang streets.

## Oriental Pearl Tower

Sources:

- [Official Shanghai Oriental Pearl listing](https://www.meet-in-shanghai.net/en/pudong-new-area/the-oriental-pearl-tv-tower-341119/)
- [Real skyline photograph and camera metadata](https://explorecams.com/photos/FLa5wmsKuB?model=ex-z35)

Traits carried into the art study:

- Three splayed structural supports around the central core.
- Large lower sphere and smaller upper spheres with clearly separated decks.
- Slender antenna that remains readable above the surrounding skyline.
- Plaza at the base and immediate relationship with the Huangpu River.
- Muted pearl-red spheres, warm structural columns, and cool glass towers around it.
